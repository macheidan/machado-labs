Macheidan Labs Publisher

Internal publishing tool of Macheidan Labs. It uploads our own finished videos to the YouTube channels and TikTok accounts we own and operate, using the official YouTube Data API v3 (YouTube API Services) and the TikTok Content Posting API.

What it does on YouTube

  • The channel owner signs in with Google and authorizes the app (OAuth 2.0, scopes youtube.upload and youtube).
  • Uploads the finished video (videos.insert), sets title, description, privacy status and publish time, and the custom thumbnail (thumbnails.set).
  • Lists the channel's own uploads to confirm the result (videos.list, channels.list).

No data from viewers or other users is collected. The tool is not available to the public.

By using this tool with YouTube you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google data is handled according to the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be revoked at any time at security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
